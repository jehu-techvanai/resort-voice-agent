# About Palm Lagoon Resort
Palm Lagoon Resort is a 4-star beach resort in Kovalam, Kerala, about 16 km (around 30 minutes by car) from Trivandrum International Airport. It sits on a private stretch of Lighthouse Beach and has 64 rooms and villas set in 6 acres of coconut gardens. The resort is open all year. Reception is open 24 hours. Phone: +91 471 000 0000. Email: stay@palmlagoon.example.

# Check-in and check-out
Check-in time is 2:00 PM and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Early check-in from 10:00 AM is free if the room is available, otherwise it costs 50 percent of one night. Late check-out until 3:00 PM costs 1,500 rupees, subject to availability. A valid government photo ID is required for every adult guest at check-in. Foreign nationals must show a passport and valid visa.

# Room type: Garden View Room
The Garden View Room is 32 square metres with a king bed or twin beds, a private sit-out facing the gardens, air conditioning, a rain shower, a 43-inch smart TV, a minibar and a tea and coffee maker. It sleeps 2 adults and 1 child under 12. There are 30 Garden View Rooms. Price: 6,500 rupees per night in low season and 8,500 rupees per night in peak season, for two adults including breakfast.

# Room type: Sea View Deluxe Room
The Sea View Deluxe Room is 40 square metres with a king bed, a private balcony facing the Arabian Sea, a bathtub and rain shower, a work desk, a 55-inch smart TV, a minibar and a Nespresso machine. It sleeps 2 adults and 1 child under 12. There are 20 Sea View Deluxe Rooms. Price: 9,500 rupees per night in low season and 12,500 rupees per night in peak season, for two adults including breakfast.

# Room type: Pool Villa
The Pool Villa is 85 square metres, a standalone villa with a private plunge pool, an outdoor shower, a king bed, a separate living area and a sofa bed. It sleeps 3 adults or 2 adults and 2 children. There are 10 Pool Villas. Price: 18,000 rupees per night in low season and 24,000 rupees per night in peak season, including breakfast and evening cocktails.

# Room type: Family Suite
The Family Suite is 70 square metres with two connected bedrooms (one king, one twin), two bathrooms and a garden-facing living room. It sleeps 4 adults or 2 adults and 3 children. There are 4 Family Suites. Price: 15,000 rupees per night in low season and 19,500 rupees per night in peak season, for up to four guests including breakfast.

# Seasons, taxes and extra guests
Peak season runs from 15 December to 15 January. All other dates are low season. All room prices are exclusive of GST, which is charged at 18 percent on rooms above 7,500 rupees per night and 12 percent below that. An extra adult costs 2,500 rupees per night including breakfast. Children under 6 stay free. Children aged 6 to 12 cost 1,200 rupees per night including breakfast. An extra bed is available in Sea View Deluxe Rooms and Family Suites only.

# Meal plans
Breakfast is included with every room. Half board (breakfast and dinner) costs 2,200 rupees per adult per night extra. Full board (all meals) costs 3,800 rupees per adult per night extra. Children aged 6 to 12 pay half the meal plan price.

# Restaurants and bar
Thira is the all-day restaurant serving Kerala, Indian and continental food from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM. Breakfast is served from 7:00 AM to 10:30 AM. Kadal is the beachfront seafood grill, open for dinner only from 7:00 PM to 10:30 PM, and reservations are recommended. The Sunset Bar by the pool is open from 11:00 AM to 11:30 PM. Vegetarian, vegan, Jain and gluten-free meals are available on request. In-room dining is available 24 hours.

# Facilities
The resort has an infinity pool facing the sea open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM, a separate kids' pool, a 24-hour gym, a yoga pavilion with a free morning yoga class at 7:00 AM daily, a kids' club for ages 4 to 12 open from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, free high-speed Wi-Fi everywhere, free parking, and a business centre. The beach has lifeguards from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

# Ayurveda spa
Sukham Ayurveda Spa is open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM and has a resident Ayurveda doctor. Popular treatments include Abhyanga full body massage for 60 minutes at 3,500 rupees, Shirodhara for 45 minutes at 4,200 rupees, and a foot reflexology session for 30 minutes at 1,800 rupees. Multi-day Panchakarma packages of 7, 14 and 21 days are available on request after a consultation with the doctor. Please book spa treatments at least 4 hours in advance.

# Activities and excursions
The resort arranges backwater houseboat day trips to Poovar for 4,500 rupees per person, a Trivandrum city tour covering Padmanabhaswamy Temple and the Napier Museum for 2,500 rupees per person, catamaran sunrise rides for 1,500 rupees per person, Kathakali performances every Friday evening in the amphitheatre free for guests, and cooking classes on Kerala cuisine every Tuesday and Saturday for 1,800 rupees per person.

# Airport transfers
Airport pickup or drop by sedan costs 1,200 rupees one way and by SUV costs 1,800 rupees one way. Pool Villa guests get a free airport transfer both ways. Please share flight details at least 24 hours before arrival.

# Booking, deposit and cancellation
Bookings can be made on the website, by phone or by email. A deposit of one night's stay is needed to confirm the booking. Free cancellation is allowed up to 7 days before arrival. Cancellations within 7 days are charged one night. No-shows are charged the full stay. During peak season, cancellations within 21 days are charged 50 percent of the full stay. Payment is accepted by credit card, debit card, UPI and bank transfer.

# Policies
Pets are not allowed, except assistance dogs. Smoking is not allowed in rooms; there is a designated smoking area near the parking. Outside food and drink are not permitted in public areas. Quiet hours are from 10:30 PM to 7:00 AM. Couples must show ID; unmarried couples are welcome. The resort is wheelchair accessible, and 2 Garden View Rooms are fully accessible with roll-in showers.

# Weddings and events
The beach lawn holds up to 250 guests and the Lagoon Hall up to 120 guests. Destination wedding packages start from 6 lakh rupees for 100 guests, excluding rooms. The events team can be reached at events@palmlagoon.example.
